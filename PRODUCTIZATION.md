# AI Receptionist Pro — Productization Track

This branch is the productization track for AI Receptionist Pro.

## Product promise
A white-label, multi-sector AI receptionist that handles inbound WhatsApp conversations, answers business questions, qualifies requests, checks availability, books appointments, and escalates to humans when required.

## Launch presets
- Salon & Barber
- Beauty & Wellness
- Dental & Clinic (administrative scheduling only)
- Veterinary
- Gym & Fitness
- Auto Service
- Consultant / Professional Services

## Commercial packaging
The product is packaged with deployment documentation, environment configuration guidance, sector presets, branding controls, security notes, test procedures, and an MIT attribution notice. Customers provide their own third-party credentials.

## Priorities
1. Security and tenant isolation
2. Resource/staff-aware booking
3. White-label branding
4. Usage controls
5. Sector presets and onboarding
6. Production deployment and QA
